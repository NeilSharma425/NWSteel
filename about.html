About Northwest Steel & Pipe

Over 75 years of excellence in steel distribution and processing

Who We Are

Our Story

Founded in Tacoma in 1950 by Jay Wax and Floyd Lamken, Northwest Steel & Pipe Inc. began as a small scrap metal and salvage yard and has grown into one of the Pacific Northwest's most trusted steel service centers.

Through the 1960s–1990s, we expanded our inventory and facilities, transitioning from scrap metal to new steel distribution. Today, we're a third-generation family-owned business operating from a modern 160,000 sq ft warehouse on a 15-acre site in South Tacoma.

Our commitment to customer service, technical know-how, and continued growth has made us the go-to steel supplier for contractors, fabricators, and manufacturers throughout Washington and Oregon. We provide daily deliveries along the entire I-5 corridor and maintain an extensive inventory of structural steel products.
