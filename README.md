# Northwest Steel & Pipe Inc. Website

A modern, clean, and professional website redesign for Northwest Steel & Pipe Inc. - a family-owned full-line steel service center serving the Pacific Northwest since 1950.

## About the Company

Northwest Steel & Pipe Inc. was founded in Tacoma in 1950 by Jay Wax and Floyd Lamken as a small scrap metal and salvage yard. Today, it's a third-generation family-owned business operating from a modern 160,000 sq ft warehouse on a 15-acre site in South Tacoma, providing daily deliveries along the I-5 corridor from Bellingham to Eugene.

## Features

- **Modern Design**: Clean, minimal design with professional industrial aesthetics
- **Fully Responsive**: Optimized for all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Smooth Animations**: Engaging scroll animations and transitions
- **Interactive Navigation**: Sticky navigation with active link highlighting
- **Contact Form**: Fully validated contact form with user feedback
- **Performance Optimized**: Fast loading times and smooth scrolling
- **Accessibility**: Keyboard navigation support and proper ARIA labels

## Website Sections

1. **Hero Section**: Highlighting 75+ years of family-owned service
2. **About Section**: Company history from 1950 to present, with key statistics
3. **Services Section**: Complete processing capabilities including:
   - High Definition Plasma Cutting (CNC controlled, Messer equipment)
   - Flame Cutting (beveling capabilities)
   - Plate Drilling (up to 4″ diameter)
   - Plate Shearing (16 gauge to ½″ thick, up to 144″ long)
   - Precision Sawing (6 band saws, miter cuts up to 60°)
   - Ironworker Services (Geka Hydra Crop 110)
   - Trucking & Delivery (I-5 corridor coverage)
4. **Inventory Section**: Comprehensive steel product catalog including:
   - Structural shapes (Angles, I-Beams, Channels, Tees)
   - Bars & rounds (Flat, Round, Square, Rebar)
   - Plate & sheet (Steel plate, Sheet metal, Coil, Expanded metal)
   - Pipe & tube (Schedule 40/80, Square, Rectangular, Round tubing)
   - Specialty items (Rail cap, Grating, Custom cuts)
5. **Contact Section**: Real business information and inquiry form
6. **Footer**: Quick links, services, and contact information
