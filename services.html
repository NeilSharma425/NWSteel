Processing Services

State-of-the-art equipment and expert technicians for precision steel processing

What We Do

Our Capabilities

Comprehensive steel processing services with modern equipment and experienced operators

High Definition Plasma Cutting

CNC controlled high-definition plasma and flame cutting with beveling capabilities. We work from .dwg/.dxf files and prints with multiple Messer cutting machines. Perfect for complex shapes and tight tolerances.

  • CNC controlled for precision
  • Messer cutting equipment
  • Beveling capabilities
  • .dwg/.dxf file support

Flame Cutting

Precision flame cutting services using advanced Messer equipment with plasma and flame torches for accurate cuts on heavy plate materials. Ideal for thick steel plate applications.

  • Heavy plate cutting
  • Messer plasma/flame torches
  • High accuracy cuts
  • Thick material capability

Plate Drilling

Professional plate drilling services with capacity up to 4″ diameter holes. Precision drilling for all your structural steel fabrication needs with accurate hole placement and clean finishes.

  • Up to 4″ diameter capacity
  • Precision hole placement
  • Clean, accurate holes
  • Multiple hole patterns

Plate Shearing

Wysong plate shear capable of cutting from 16 gauge up to ½″ thick and up to 144″ long for precise, clean plate cuts. Perfect for straight cuts on sheet and plate materials.

  • 16 gauge to ½″ thick
  • Up to 144″ length
  • Wysong shear equipment
  • Clean, straight cuts

Precision Sawing

Six band saws for structural shapes and tubing with automated cut watchers and laser systems. Capable of miter cuts up to 60° for accurate angles. Fast, accurate cutting for production runs.

  • Six automated band saws
  • Laser positioning systems
  • Miter cuts up to 60°
  • Structural shapes & tubing

Ironworker Services

Geka Hydra Crop 110 ironworker for efficient shearing and punching of angles, flats, and other structural shapes. Quick turnaround for notching, punching, and shearing operations.

  • Geka Hydra Crop 110
  • Shearing & punching
  • Angles & flats
  • Fast turnaround

Trucking & Delivery

Daily deliveries along the I-5 corridor from Bellingham to Eugene, including Tacoma, Seattle, Everett, Olympia, Portland, Vancouver, and all points between. Reliable delivery you can count on.

  • Daily I-5 corridor service
  • Bellingham to Eugene
  • Reliable scheduling
  • Professional drivers
