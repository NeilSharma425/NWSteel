Who We Are

About Northwest Steel & Pipe

Founded in Tacoma in 1950 by Jay Wax and Floyd Lamken, Northwest Steel & Pipe Inc. began as a small scrap metal and salvage yard and has grown into one of the Pacific Northwest's most trusted steel service centers.

Through the 1960s–1990s, we expanded our inventory and facilities, transitioning from scrap metal to new steel distribution. Today, we're a third-generation family-owned business operating from a modern 160,000 sq ft warehouse on a 15-acre site in South Tacoma.

Our commitment to customer service, technical know-how, and continued growth has made us the go-to steel supplier for contractors, fabricators, and manufacturers throughout Washington and Oregon. We provide daily deliveries along the entire I-5 corridor and maintain an extensive inventory of structural steel products.

What We Do

Processing Services

State-of-the-art steel processing capabilities with precision and reliability

High Definition Plasma Cutting

CNC controlled high-definition plasma and flame cutting with beveling capabilities. We work from .dwg/.dxf files and prints with multiple Messer cutting machines.

Flame Cutting

Precision flame cutting services using advanced Messer equipment with plasma and flame torches for accurate cuts on heavy plate materials.

Plate Drilling

Professional plate drilling services with capacity up to 4″ diameter holes. Precision drilling for all your structural steel fabrication needs.

Plate Shearing

Wysong plate shear capable of cutting from 16 gauge up to ½″ thick and up to 144″ long for precise, clean plate cuts.

Precision Sawing

Six band saws for structural shapes and tubing with automated cut watchers and laser systems. Capable of miter cuts up to 60° for accurate angles.

Ironworker Services

Geka Hydra Crop 110 ironworker for efficient shearing and punching of angles, flats, and other structural shapes.

Trucking & Delivery

Daily deliveries along the I-5 corridor from Bellingham to Eugene, including Tacoma, Seattle, Everett, Olympia, Portland, Vancouver, and all points between.
