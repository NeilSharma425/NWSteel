Welcome to Northwest Steel & Pipe

Quality Steel Products & Services Since 1950

For over 75 years, Northwest Steel & Pipe Inc. has been the Pacific Northwest's trusted partner for structural steel products and precision processing services.

Founded in Tacoma in 1950, we've grown from a small scrap metal yard into a comprehensive steel service center with a modern 160,000 sq ft facility. As a third-generation family-owned business, we combine decades of experience with state-of-the-art equipment to serve contractors, fabricators, and manufacturers throughout Washington and Oregon.
